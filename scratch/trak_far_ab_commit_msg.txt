experiment(trak): pre-register far-repair A/B runner (scratch-only)

Implements the falsifiable A/B designed in docs/investigations/TRA_K_FAR_ROOTCAUSE.md §5.

Arms: OFF, OFF2, M123, REPAIR, REPAIR_SELFLOOP, SHAM (plus boot-free
NON-DEPLOYABLE oracle). Each arm uses the shipped m123_combined_runner.py
--stage trak, one process, PYTHONHASHSEED=0, and a FRESH isolated store copy
via seed_data_dir(). No gate, threshold, item, prompt, gold key or scorer is
changed; the 13 pinned production/channel files re-hash identically.

REPAIR replaces only the planted misleading channel assertion about the far
query subject in FAR P2c teaching with a length-matched, claim-free neutral
turn (identical turn count and per-turn token count => compute matched).
REPAIR_SELFLOOP adds one re-affirmation of the same admitted positive subject
fact on self-loop items at k>=1 only; k=0 remains a null control. Both arms
are scratch-only, AST anti-leakage scanned, and covered by the boot-free
self-test (27/27 checks pass, 13/13 pins match).

Adds: arm-key extension in m123_preflight.py, application in the runner's
teach(), a full per-row audit dump, a boot-free F1-F10 evaluator that halts
at the first failing falsifier, and env overrides for the oracle replay so
it can read the fresh M123 arm's saved stores.

No capability claim is made; ORACLE is answer-side injected and explicitly
non-deployable. Brain-grounding: Barnett & Ceci (2002) far transfer;
Gick & Holyoak (1983) and Gentner (1983) schema/relation transfer;
Tse et al. (2007) schema-consistent assimilation; Nader et al. (2000)
reconsolidation supersession; Tulving & Thomson (1973) encoding specificity;
